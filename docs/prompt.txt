Design a single-floor, north-facing Vastu-compliant home that maximizes usable
built area on a rectangular plot, 52 ft (East–West, road frontage) × 76 ft
(North–South, depth). Space efficiency is a hard priority: minimize every
unassigned, decorative, or leftover area within the building footprint.
Orientation: North = top, East = right, South = bottom, West = left.
Every item below is a hard requirement. Do not add or drop rooms.

PLOT / ENVELOPE — MAXIMIZED
- Use code-minimum setbacks only: 5 ft front (north), 3 ft rear (south),
  3 ft each side (east/west). Buildable envelope ≈ 46 ft × 68 ft = 3,128 sq ft.
- Target ground coverage: 68–72% of plot area for the built footprint
  (≈ 2,690–2,845 sq ft), consistent with typical municipal FSI/coverage caps —
  do not push past 72% even if the layout allows it.
- Wall standard: 9 in exterior, 4 in interior partitions (thinner partitions
  recover floor area vs. the standard 4.5 in, while remaining structurally
  normal for load-bearing-light interior walls).
- Use a 6-inch coordinate grid for all placement so the layout tiles without
  gaps: no strip of floor area may be narrower than 2 ft or unassigned to any
  room, corridor, or wall.
- Any space between the building footprint and the setback line is exterior
  margin only (driveway/garden) — not part of this brief, not a room.

ROOMS (exactly these 10; dimensions may flex ±1 ft per side, elastically,
specifically to consume any leftover grid space so no gaps remain — do not
leave a room at its minimum size if adjoining unassigned space could instead
be folded into it)
1. Master bedroom — SW, ~14×15 ft (flex up to 15×16 ft if adjacent space allows),
   attached master bathroom.
2. Bedroom 2 — NW, ~11×12 ft, for elderly parents: step-free threshold,
   door width ≥3 ft.
3. Bedroom 3 — W (between Bedroom 2 and Master Bedroom), ~11×12 ft.
4. Bedroom 4 — S / south-central, between Master Bedroom and Kitchen/Dining,
   ~11×12 ft, on the south exterior wall.
5. Master bathroom — ~6×8 ft, entered only from the master bedroom, tucked
   against the bedroom's north-west internal wall (avoid extreme SW corner).
6. Common bathroom — ~6×7 ft, west-central, entered only from the circulation
   spine (never through a bedroom); share a plumbing wall with the master
   bathroom to shorten pipe runs and let both bathrooms nest into one compact
   wet-zone block instead of two separated ones.
7. Living room — center-north, ~15×18 ft (flex to fill remaining north-central
   width), extending east along its southern portion to meet the kitchen.
8. Kitchen — SE, ~11×13 ft, open (no wall) along its shared edge with living.
9. Dining area — adjacent to kitchen (south or west side), ~10×12 ft
   (flex to absorb any leftover SE-zone space), direct door to kitchen.
10. Puja room — NE, ~6×7 ft, corner alcove off the living room's east
    extension, sized to fully close the NE corner (no sliver left beside it).

CIRCULATION — MINIMIZED
- One private circulation spine, ≤3 ft wide (step-free, still ≥3 ft clear per
  Bedroom 2's accessibility requirement), branching once from the living room.
  All four bedroom doors and the common bathroom door open off this spine.
- The spine's footprint counts against the coverage target above — do not
  oversize it "for comfort"; 3 ft is the ceiling, not a starting point.
- No bedroom may be a through-passage to any other room.
- Every room must be reachable from the main entrance via the living room
  and/or spine.
- Do not add any secondary corridor, landing, or buffer zone beyond this
  single spine.

GATES AND DOORS
- Exactly one main entrance gate, north exterior wall, into the living room,
  centered on the living room's north wall, ≥2 ft clear of each corner.
- Door widths: 3 ft bedrooms/bathrooms/kitchen-dining; entrance 3.5 ft.
- Internal doors: living room ↔ spine; spine ↔ each bedroom; master bedroom
  → master bath (only entry); spine → common bathroom; kitchen ↔ dining.
  Living room ↔ kitchen = open boundary, no door.
- No door at a room corner (≥2 ft clearance both sides); no two openings on
  the same wall may overlap (≥6 in gap).

WINDOWS / VENTILATION
- Every bedroom, both bathrooms, living room, kitchen, and dining area must
  touch an exterior wall with ≥1 ventilation window.
- Minimum widths: 3.5 ft bedrooms/living/kitchen/dining; 1.5 ft bathrooms
  (frosted, high-sill).
- A window and the entrance gate must never share the same wall interval.
- Where two rooms both need exterior wall footage in a tight zone (e.g.
  Bedroom 4 and Dining both wanting south-facing wall), split the wall length
  proportionally rather than shrinking either room's minimum window width.

PRIVACY
- The sleeping wing (all bedrooms + attached bath) must stay outside the
  direct sightline from the entrance/living room, even with the tightened
  spine.

VASTU ZONES (hard): Master bedroom SW, Bedroom 2 NW, Kitchen SE, Puja NE.

SPACE-EFFICIENCY CHECK (apply after initial layout)
- Sum all room + spine + wall areas; compare to the built footprint. Any
  unassigned residual over 20 sq ft must be resolved by flexing an adjoining
  room's dimension (within its stated tolerance) to absorb it — not left as
  open floor space.

EXCLUDED: foyer, utility/laundry, storage room, office, balcony, garage, and
any bedroom or bathroom beyond the ten rooms listed above.














Design a single-floor, north-facing Vastu-compliant home, 52 ft (E–W) × 76 ft

floor-plan generator can satisfy it directly — no requirement should need
its own separate sub-calculation to check.

SITE
- Plot area: 3,952 sq ft.
- Setbacks: 8 ft front (north), 8 ft rear (south), 5 ft each side (east/west).
- Buildable footprint: approx. 42 ft × 60 ft = 2,520 sq ft (about 64% of plot).
- This footprint comfortably fits the 10 rooms below with room to spare —
  do not shrink the footprint further, and do not feel pressure to fill
  100% of it; some open margin inside the footprint is fine.

ROOMS (exactly these 10; sizes are approximate, ±1 ft per side is fine)
1. Master bedroom — SW — about 15×16 ft. Attached master bathroom.
2. Bedroom 2 — NW — about 12×13 ft. For elderly parents: no-step entry,
   door at least 3 ft wide.
3. Bedroom 3 — W — about 12×13 ft.
4. Bedroom 4 — south side — about 12×13 ft.
5. Master bathroom — about 6×8 ft. Only entered from the master bedroom.
6. Common bathroom — about 6×7 ft. Only entered from the shared
   circulation area, never through a bedroom.
7. Living room — center-north — about 16×19 ft, near the main entrance.
8. Kitchen — SE — about 12×14 ft, open (no dividing wall) to the living room.
9. Dining area — next to the kitchen — about 11×13 ft, with a door to the kitchen.
10. Puja room — NE — about 6×7 ft.

CIRCULATION

  layout allows — this is the only corridor permitted.
- No bedroom may be used as a passage to reach another room.

- Keep the bedrooms out of direct view from the entrance.

DOORS

- Each bedroom has one door onto the shared passage.

  must have at least one window on an exterior wall.
- The entrance and a window should not sit on the same wall section.

VASTU ZONES (hard): Master bedroom SW, Bedroom 2 NW, Kitchen SE, Puja NE.

EXCLUDED: foyer, utility/laundry, storage room, office, balcony, garage,
and any bedroom or bathroom beyond the 10 listed above.

CENTER OF THE PLAN

- The living room and master bedroom, being the largest rooms, should each

- The entrance and a window should not sit on the same wall section.

KITCHEN NOTE

Design a two-storey (G+1), north-facing, Vastu-compliant home on a rectangular
plot of 30 ft (East-West, road frontage) x 36 ft (North-South, depth) —
1,080 sq ft footprint per floor, ~2,160 sq ft total built-up area.
North = top, East = right, South = bottom, West = left.
No setbacks: the building line coincides with the plot boundary on all
four sides.

NOTE: this is a conceptual/idealized brief. Real construction in India is
normally governed by local Development Control Regulations (DCR), which
mandate minimum setbacks (commonly 3-5 ft front, 3 ft sides/rear). Treat
"no setbacks" as a deliberate design simplification, not a real-world
exemption — flag this if the design is ever taken to actual construction.

STRUCTURAL & CONSTRUCTION ASSUMPTIONS
- RCC framed structure (columns + beams); internal partitions are non-load-bearing.
- External wall thickness: 9 in (230 mm). Internal partition thickness: 4.5 in (115 mm).
- Floor-to-floor height: 10 ft (9 ft clear + ~12 in slab/beam).
- Staircase: dog-leg with mid-landing, 2 flights, flight width 3.5 ft,
  riser 7 in / tread 10 in (~16-17 risers total), min headroom 7 ft.
  MUST occupy the identical footprint on both floors (structural continuity),
  located in the south-central zone — clear of the NE (Puja) corner and the
  north-center (Living) zone.
- Minimum window area per habitable room: ~1/8 of that room's floor area.
  Main door: 3'6" x 7'. Internal doors: 2'6"-3' x 7'. Bathroom doors: 2'3" x 7'.
- Common bathrooms on both floors should be vertically aligned (or close to
  it) to keep plumbing stacks simple.
